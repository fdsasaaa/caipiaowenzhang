## Targeted Fixes

The seven failures were addressed without lowering the acceptance threshold:

1. Purely negative safety statements about profit / win rate / hit rate were prevented from being promoted into unsupported factual hard claims.
2. Exact machine-pipeline calculations were given deterministic evidence completion only when they matched the frozen pipeline; unrelated or malformed evidence remains fail-closed.
3. Negative statements such as “this is not an odds / profit conclusion” were separated from actual provider-economics claims, which still require verified economics evidence.

Reverse tests were retained so that affirmative claims such as unsupported win-rate, hit-rate, profit, odds, or non-pipeline evidence continue to fail.
